# Privacy Policy — PortPilot

Last updated: 13 August 2026

PortPilot is a desktop app that runs on your computer. It is made by Shailesh Jha.

## What we collect

PortPilot does **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or account information. There is no PortPilot account.

Data you enter (port lists, clipboard history, database connection names, saved queries, settings) stays in local app storage on your machine. Database passwords are stored in the operating system keychain (or in memory for the current session if you turn off “Save password for next launch”).

## Network

The app talks to:

- Hosts and databases **you** configure (when you connect)
- **GitHub Releases**, to check for updates

We do not sell data. We do not use data for tracking across apps or websites.

## Your control

Delete local data with **Settings → Safety → Erase all PortPilot data**, or uninstall (Windows uninstall removes app data; on Mac, use Erase or `resources/uninstall-macos.sh`).
